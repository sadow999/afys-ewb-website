Become a partner

We are actively seeking new collaborations with organisations that share our dedication to creating a sustainable impact. Partnering with AFYS and EWB connects scientific innovation, strategic financing and community action against some of the region's most pressing challenges.

Whether through funding, technical expertise or programmatic collaboration, your support helps scale our solutions and reach more young scientists.
