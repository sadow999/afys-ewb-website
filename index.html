Two organisations, one mandate

AFYS generates the science and the people. EWB carries it into national water policy and finance.

Arab Foundation of Young Scientists

A public benefit foundation bridging scientific research and societal impact, through networking, capacity building and applied R&D for young Arab scientists.

Explore AFYS

Egyptian Water Bank

Planning and managing water resources and financing water projects, working towards SDG 6 and clean water access for every Egyptian.

Flagship initiatives

Large-scale projects the foundation is building with government, industry and academic partners.

Al-Nama University for Science and Technology

Sohag · In development

An integrated university of ten scientific and applied faculties on a 127,157 m² site in New Sohag, built to serve Upper Egypt. Legal and technical requirements are complete, pending the presidential decree of establishment.

Zagazig International Technological University

Sharqia · In development

Converting the existing technological faculty into an applied, industry-facing university under a three-way partnership between a national academic body, an industrial funder and an international accreditation partner.

Suez Canal Economic Zone Workforce Training Centre

West Qantara · Launching 2026

A multi-disciplinary training platform steered by AFYS with Virginia Tech's Center for International Research in Education and Development and the Suez Canal Economic Zone, opening with two-week certification cycles in textile manufacturing.

Young Scientists Knowledge Economy Company

Egypt · Established

An Egyptian joint-stock company that commercialises research results, incubates start-ups founded by young scientists, and delivers engineering and environmental consultancy alongside training and innovation events.

Leadership

Prof. Dr. Alaa Abdallah El-Sadek

President, Board of Trustees

Founder of both the Arab Foundation for Young Scientists and the Egyptian Water Bank, Prof. Dr. El-Sadek connects scientific potential with strategic implementation. His expertise in water resource management and his commitment to empowering young researchers drive the dual mission of the two organisations.

He chairs the steering committee of the Suez Canal Economic Zone Workforce Training Centre alongside Virginia Tech and the Suez Canal Economic Zone, and has described the foundation's origins as inspired by the example of Dr. Ahmed Zewail.
